A Sesquicentennial Milestone of Pride & Honor

"It is with profound gratitude and immense pride that we celebrate the 150th anniversary of Prince of Wales’ College, Moratuwa. Established in 1876 through the magnificent generosity of Sir Charles Henry de Soysa, our esteemed institution has stood as an unyielding bastion of knowledge, character, and discipline."

"Over the past century and a half, generations of Cambrians have walked these historic halls, carrying forward our sacred mottos 'Ich Dien' and 'Nihil Per Saltum' into every corner of the globe. As we mark this landmark sesquicentennial year, we honor our pioneers, celebrate our current achievements, and lay down a visionary path for the future."

Our Storied Heritage

From 1876 to 2026: 150 Years of History

1876

Foundation by Sir Charles Henry de Soysa

Following the royal visit of the Prince of Wales in 1875, the greatest philanthropist in Ceylonese history, Sir Charles Henry de Soysa, established twin colleges in Moratuwa, opening on September 14, 1876.

1933

Birth of the "Battle of the Golds"

The inauguration of the annual big match cricket encounter against St. Sebastian’s College, Moratuwa. This legendary fixture has since grown into one of the country's most vibrant sporting spectacles.

2026

The Sesquicentennial Celebration

Celebrating 150 glorious years of molding exceptional young men, producing national leaders, cricketers, scholars, and professionals who serve across the globe under the banner of 'Ich Dien'.
